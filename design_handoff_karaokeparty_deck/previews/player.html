The player is another route of the same app, run fullscreen on whatever machine drives the room’s audio. Everything is sized against the stage because the audience is across the room. Overlays that sit over playing video are solid corner panels in the top-right, keeping the lower two-thirds clear for lyrics. A queue-depth VU meter runs along the bottom edge, so the room can see how long the list is without asking. The media layer is a stand-in: the real player decodes MP3+G lyric frames, plays MP4, or runs a visualizer.
