```jsx
<PlayerHeadline tone="vu">Dana</PlayerHeadline>
<PlayerHeadline size="var(--display-xl)" tone="vu">9</PlayerHeadline>
```

Michroma is wide and slow to read, so keep it to three words maximum and size it in `vh` — the audience is across the room. Amber for what the room should act on, ink for what it should just read. No animation, no colour cycling.
